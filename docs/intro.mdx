## Browser tabs

When the user first loads the page, the tab for their browser will be selected. However, if they change their selection, then that selection will be preserved on reload (which is the default Docusaurus behavior for [Tabs](https://docusaurus.io/docs/markdown-features/tabs)). To verify that this is working correctly, clear your `LocalStorage`.
